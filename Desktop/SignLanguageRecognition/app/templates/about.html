About the project

About the MSL Recognition System

This final-year project explores how computer vision and machine learning can support the recognition of static Malawi Sign Language gestures from a standard webcam. It combines a privacy-conscious dataset workflow, participant-independent model evaluation and a clear human validation process.

What the system can do

  • Detect one or two hands and extract 21 landmarks per hand.
  • Collect consented landmark samples and manage the MSL vocabulary.
  • Train, compare and publish machine-learning models with evaluation reports.
  • Show confidence-scored predictions and export personal recognition history.

Important scope

The prototype recognises individual static gestures only. It does not translate full sentences, dynamic movement or facial expressions, and it is not intended for safety-critical communication.

A sign becomes available for recognition only after a competent Malawi Sign Language signer or knowledgeable instructor has verified it.
